The work behind the title

Less noise. Better decisions.

My role is not only to find a number. It is to understand what the number means: what is included, what is missing, where the risk sits, and what the project can realistically absorb.

“A good commercial decision is an engineering decision with the assumptions made visible.”
My working principle
for procurement and estimation
01 / READ

Translate scope

Review drawings, specifications and BOQs until the actual requirement is clear — not just the item description.

02 / TEST

Test the number

Compare supplier offers on a like-for-like basis, surface exclusions, and separate price movement from scope movement.

03 / PROTECT

Protect delivery

Bring lead time, availability, technical compliance and commercial risk into the conversation before the purchase order.

04 / CLOSE

Make it usable

Give the team a clear recommendation, a clean record of assumptions, and a next step someone can act on.
